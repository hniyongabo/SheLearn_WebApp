Our Mission

To bridge the gap between education and the job market for African women in tech. Despite Africa leading with 47% of women STEM graduates, only a small fraction secure tech roles. We are here to change that. SheLearn provides a targeted, skill-based digital space that enable you to thrive in the tech workforce.

What We Do

We provide the "ladder" for women to reach the heights of the tech industry through a community-driven approach:

  • A class with a professional in tech
  • Materials to keep you engaged
  • Professional community
