SheLearn is a welcoming, encouraging space designed specifically for women. Whether you are writing your very first line of code or advancing your career, we provide the community and tools you need. No prior experience required—just bring your ambition.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between education and the job market for African women in tech. Despite Africa leading with 47% of women STEM graduates, only a small fraction secure tech roles. SheLearn is here to change that—by providing structured, skill-based learning with community support.

What We Do

We provide the “ladder” for women to reach the heights of the tech industry through a community-driven approach:

  • Live learning with professionals in tech
  • Practical materials to keep you engaged
  • A supportive, career-minded community
