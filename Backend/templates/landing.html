Our Mission

To bridge the gap between education and the job market for African women in tech.

Despite Africa leading with 47% of women STEM graduates, only a small fraction secure tech roles.

SheLearn is here to change that—by providing structured, skill-based learning with community support.

What We Do

We provide the "ladder" for women to reach the heights of the tech industry

  • Learn important concepts and practical skills in tech
  • Real-world examples after each lesson
  • A supportive, career-minded space made for you
